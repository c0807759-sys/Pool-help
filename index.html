🎱 Pool Drill Tracker

Pick a drill, choose 5, 10 or 15 shots, and your phone tracks every shot: makes, aim, cue ball position and speed.

How to set up your phone
  • Put the phone on a tripod, shelf or stand where it can see the table. It does not need to be overhead. Higher is better.
  • The first time, the app has you take a photo and tap the corner pockets. That's how it works out the camera's height and angle.
  • If the phone can't see the whole table, choose Half table and use the end it can see.
  • Use a solid colored object ball (not a stripe) so it isn't confused with the cue ball.
  • Don't move the phone during a session. Next time, the app helps you line it back up with your saved photo.

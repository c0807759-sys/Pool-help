🎱 Pool Drill Tracker

Pick a drill, aim your phone at the table, and it counts your makes.

Camera setup
  • Mount the phone on a tripod, shelf or light fixture. It doesn't need to be overhead. If it can't see the whole table, pick Half table when calibrating.
  • Higher is better. A steep angle squashes the far end, and the app will warn you if that end gets too small to track.
  • 🔄 Front / Back switches cameras. ⇄ Mirror drill moves the drill to the other end of the table.
  • After calibrating, don't move the phone. If it gets bumped, tap Recalibrate.
  • Landscape works best. Plug in the phone for long sessions.
